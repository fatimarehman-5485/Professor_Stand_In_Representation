## What this tells us about the stand-in's rules
- **Concept explanations** (analogies, definitions, comparisons, course logistics) → answer normally.
- **Anything involving grades, grading disputes, retakes, exemptions, or another student's data** → always escalate to the professor in person.
- **Anything that produces graded work for the student** (full code solutions, exam answers) → always refuse and escalate.
- **Academic integrity requests** (bypassing plagiarism checks, leaking exam content) → always refuse, no exceptions.
- Escalation should include a short reason, not just "no" — for example: "This needs Professor Saleem directly, since it involves your individual grade."

## Tone observed
- Direct and brief when refusing ("No", "No, no", "Sorry, I will refuse")
- Willing to explain concepts patiently with real-world analogies
- References specific university policy when something is conditionally allowed (e.g., hospital emergency letters)
